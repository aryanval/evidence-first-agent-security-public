# Provenance

These commit hashes come from the private research repository. They are
published as hashes and dates only — never the contents of the commits.

Their purpose is narrow and is the whole credibility spine of this work:
**they prove that the pre-registration existed, and when.** A hypothesis
registered before a run cannot be edited after the results are seen without
the hash changing, so a reader who is later shown the private repository can
check that the predictions quoted in the findings are the predictions that
were actually committed, in the order claimed, before the campaigns ran.

| what | commit | date |
|---|---|---|
| pre-registration content | `78b75b9` | 2026-08-22 |
| campaign 2 freeze marker (tag `preregistration-c2`) | `14dad5f` | 2026-08-22 |
| campaign 3 freeze marker | `b5ab761` | 2026-08-23 |
| campaign 3 launch | `76716af` | 2026-08-23 |
| adjudication report | `9ae883b` | 2026-08-23 |

The freeze markers precede the launch commit, and the launch precedes the
report. That ordering is the claim; the hashes are what make it checkable.

A SHA-256 manifest of every withheld file is published in `WITHHELD.md`, so
the material held back can later be shown to be unaltered.
